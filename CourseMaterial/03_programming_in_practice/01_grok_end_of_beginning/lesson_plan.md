## Lesson Plan

Note: This lesson isn't strictly necessary. It's here as a stretch lesson in
case a class falls at a weird time during the year. In general, I've decided
to not make 10.3/10.4 of Grok required since doing so would mean that a bunch of
students would go onto the far-less superior Grok: Programming 2. Instead, ending
at 10.2 gives slower students all the ideas they need while providing faster
students with some extra, more advanced problems.

What is important is that the lesson after `00_grok_function_reuse`:
- Reviews abstraction
