- What is the best way to learn from documentation?
- What does it feel like to be learning?

## Lesson Plan

In this lesson, students will be given the chance to reflect on everything that
they've done since the start of the year. This means giving students a chance to
reflect on how they learn and whether there is anything that they can do better
as they go through the rest of Grok, Intro to CS, and high school. The timing is
good because:

1. They just had a quiz
2. In the previous class we called back to the first unit we did in class
3. There are a lot of breaks during this part of the year

After this discussion, students work to complete the Grok lessons on 7.2.
